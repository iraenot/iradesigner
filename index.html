About

I'm a UX/UI designer with 6 years of experience creating and improving digital products across different industries.

My background in conversion optimization shapes how I approach design: I look beyond aesthetics to understand user behavior, identify friction points and uncover opportunities for improvement.

I've worked across the entire design process, from research, data analysis and UX evaluation to wireframing, prototyping and interface design. I enjoy tackling complex challenges, connecting different perspectives and turning insights into practical solutions.

I believe good design comes from understanding both the people who use a product and the goals behind it.
